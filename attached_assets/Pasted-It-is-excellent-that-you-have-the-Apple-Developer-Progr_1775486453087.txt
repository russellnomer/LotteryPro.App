As a fellow architect, I need to give you some candor right out of the gate: **You cannot compile a final iOS binary (`.ipa` file) or submit directly to the App Store entirely within Replit's native environment.** Replit runs on Linux containers, and Apple strictly requires macOS and Xcode to compile iOS applications and upload them to App Store Connect. 

Furthermore, simply wrapping the web app and pushing it to Apple will result in an **immediate App Store rejection** due to your monetization strategy. Apple requires all digital goods (your Stripe credit packs and PayPal subscriptions) to use **Apple's In-App Purchases (IAP)**, where they take a 15-30% cut. 

To get LotteryPro onto iPhones, iPads, and Mac (via Catalyst or Apple Silicon), we will use **Capacitor**. Capacitor allows you to keep your existing React/Vite SPA and package it as a native app, while giving you access to native device APIs.

### Phase 3: Compiling and Submitting (Off-Replit)
Since you have the Apple Developer account ready (as shown in your screenshot), here is how you bridge the gap between Replit and Apple:

1. **Push your Replit code to a Git Repository** (e.g., GitHub).
2. **Move to a macOS Environment:** You must clone this repository onto a Mac device.
3. **Build and Sync:**
   Run the following commands on your Mac terminal:
   * `npm install`
   * `npm run build` (Builds the Vite frontend)
   * `npx cap add ios` (Creates the Xcode project)
   * `npx cap sync` (Copies your web assets into the native iOS project)
4. **Open in Xcode:** Run `npx cap open ios`. This will open your project in Xcode.
5. **Sign the App:** In Xcode, go to the "Signing & Capabilities" tab. Log in with the Apple ID associated with the Developer Account in your screenshot. Select your Team (Russell Nomer) and let Xcode automatically manage the provisioning profiles.
6. **Archive and Upload:** In Xcode, select "Any iOS Device" as the build target, go to the menu bar -> **Product** -> **Archive**. Once archived, use the Organizer window to distribute the app to **TestFlight / App Store Connect**.
