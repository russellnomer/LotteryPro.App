Yes — this Replit version is good, and better than the earlier drafts in one important way: it now names the documented `@capacitor/inappbrowser` method `openInExternalBrowser()` explicitly, which matches the current Capacitor docs. Capacitor’s InAppBrowser plugin supports three modes, including opening a URL in the device’s default browser, and the documented example for that is `InAppBrowser.openInExternalBrowser({ url })`. By contrast, `@capacitor/browser` is explicitly an in-app browser and uses `SFSafariViewController` on iOS. ([Capacitor][1])

The compliance wording is also in a good place now. Apple says its App Review Guidelines were updated on May 1, 2025 for the U.S. storefront regarding buttons, external links, and other calls to action, so your current wording about a centralized compliance helper is safer than hardcoding one permanent iOS rule everywhere. ([Apple Developer][2])
