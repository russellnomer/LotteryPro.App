## About

90 monthly listeners

Russell Nomer creates music driven by pure emotion, not genre. He collaborates with AI to craft soundscapes reflecting his journey through accidents, near-death, and chronic pain. Denied disability, music became his voice. Lyrics and AI merge, transcending language, emphasizing feeling in every production.
